# Peekling community guidelines

Everyone is welcome here. Designers, illustrators, animators, developers, and first-time contributors can all help a new Peekling find its feet.

These are the community guidelines for `peekling/awesome-peeklings`. They apply to proposals, pull requests, reviews, Discussions, and every Pack kept in this collection.

## Bring a character you can share

Submit artwork you made or artwork you have clear permission to redistribute. Credit collaborators and source material honestly. If a generative tool helped create the artwork, disclose the tool in `RIGHTS.json` and make sure its terms allow the submitted license.

Do not submit copied characters, confusing imitations, undisclosed brand assets, or work that trades on another creator's identity. Adapted and commissioned work needs a source record and redistribution rights that reviewers can understand.

## Keep Packs data-only

A community Pack contains a manifest, PNG artwork, documentation, and license records. It does not contain scripts, executable behavior, telemetry, trackers, or files that load code from somewhere else.

Automated checks validate this boundary, the Pack structure, atlas hashes, image geometry, and declared metadata. Passing those checks does not prove authorship or make a Pack first-party Peekling software. Maintainer review remains part of acceptance.

## Make review straightforward

Keep one Peekling Pack in each pull request. Explain the character, show a preview, answer rights questions directly, and make requested changes in the same pull request. Reviewers will be specific about what needs work and why.

A submission may be declined when its rights are unclear, its character is too close to protected work, its content is harmful or deceptive, or it does not fit the collection. A decline is a curation decision, not a judgment of the contributor.

## Treat people with care

Be curious, kind, and specific. Critique the work without belittling the person. Harassment, threats, hateful content, sexualized content involving minors, doxxing, impersonation, and repeated unwanted contact are not welcome.

Respect a contributor's name, pronouns, culture, and accessibility needs. Give creators credit. Do not pressure someone to disclose private details to participate.

## Keep the collection healthy

Maintainers may update, hide, or remove a Pack when its license changes, a rights claim appears, it no longer passes current validation, or it causes harm. When practical, we will explain the reason and give the creator a path to correct the record.

Community inclusion is not permanent endorsement. The official first-party collection remains in [`peekling/peekling-characters`](https://github.com/peekling/peekling-characters).

## Report a concern

For a Pack-format problem, open an issue. For a security concern, use [GitHub's private vulnerability report](https://github.com/peekling/awesome-peeklings/security/advisories/new). For sensitive conduct or rights concerns, do not post personal details publicly. Contact the maintainer through the contact method listed at [prajwal.me](https://prajwal.me).

Maintainers will review reports in context, limit unnecessary disclosure, and act according to these guidelines.
